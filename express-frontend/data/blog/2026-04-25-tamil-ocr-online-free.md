## What is Tamil OCR?

Tamil OCR is the technology that converts images of Tamil writing into editable Tamil Unicode text. Instead of retyping a printed Tamil document by hand, you upload the image (or a photo of the page) and the OCR engine returns the Tamil characters as text. You can then paste them into Word, Google Docs, WhatsApp, an email, or a blog post.

Modern Tamil OCR uses deep learning models trained on millions of Tamil characters across many fonts and printing styles. The accuracy on clean printed text is usually above 95%; on photographs of physical books, it's still above 90% in good lighting.

## What kinds of files can Tamil OCR read?

| File type | Best for | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **JPG / JPEG** | Photos from your phone | Good lighting, no shadow, focus the camera on the text |
| **PNG** | Screenshots and high-quality scans | Keep transparent backgrounds; works great |
| **PDF** | Scanned books, multi-page documents | Tool processes page by page |
| **WEBP** | Modern image formats | Same accuracy as PNG/JPG |

For best results, the image should be at least 1000 pixels wide for a normal page of text. Below that resolution, individual Tamil characters become too pixellated for the model to read reliably.

## Common Tamil OCR mistakes (and how to fix them)

Even a 95%-accurate Tamil OCR will produce a few mistakes per page. The most common:

**ள vs ழ vs ள** — these three Tamil consonants look similar when ink is faint. The grammar checker fixes most of these in one click.

**Missing diacritics (புள்ளி, கொம்பு)** — the small dots and curves above letters can fade in scans. If you see a bare consonant where a CV-syllable should be, that's the issue.

**Joined letters at edges** — when two characters touch each other (common in cursive handwriting or condensed printing), the model may merge them into one. A quick proofread catches it.

**Numerals 1 and ௧** — Tamil-numeral OCR is hit-and-miss. If your document uses Tamil numerals, double-check them.

**Footnote / superscript text** — small text under a horizontal line is harder. Scan that section at higher resolution if accuracy matters.
