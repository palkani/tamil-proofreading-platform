## What does a Tamil grammar checker check?

A good Tamil grammar checker doesn't just look for spelling typos. It analyses your text across five layers:

### 1. Spelling (எழுத்துப்பிழை)
Wrong-letter substitutions are the most common Tamil error: writing **ள** where **ழ** belongs (or vice versa), missing the dot above **ி** vs **ீ**, mixing up **ந** vs **ன**, **ர** vs **ற**, **ல** vs **ள** vs **ழ**. A checker compares each word against a dictionary of valid Tamil forms.

### 2. Grammar (இலக்கணப் பிழை)
This includes case-suffix mismatches (e.g. wrong use of இல் vs இன்), wrong plurals, missing sandhi between adjacent words, wrong adjective forms, and broken subject–verb agreement.

### 3. Verb conjugation (வினைச்சொல் வடிவம்)
Tamil verbs change for tense, person, number, and gender — sometimes irregularly. "செய்தான்" (he did) vs "செய்தாள்" (she did) vs "செய்தனர்" (they did) — getting the wrong ending is a fast way to look careless.

### 4. Style and register (பேச்சு vs எழுத்து)
"வேணும்" is correct in everyday speech but wrong in a school essay; "வேண்டும்" is correct in formal writing but stilted in WhatsApp. A good Tamil grammar checker online free should let you pick the register.

### 5. Sandhi (புணர்ச்சி விதிகள்)
When two Tamil words combine, letters change at the boundary — "மரம் + தோட்டம்" becomes "மரத்தோட்டம்". Most native speakers feel sandhi but can't always write it correctly.

## The 7 most common Tamil writing mistakes (and quick fixes)

| # | Common mistake | Correct form | What to remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "நான் வந்தேன்னு சொன்னேன்" | "நான் வந்தேன் என்று சொன்னேன்" | "ன்னு" is colloquial; "என்று" is the standard form |
| 2 | "அவன் சாப்பிடற" | "அவன் சாப்பிடுகிறான்" | Spoken truncation drops -கிற-; written form keeps it |
| 3 | "வரது" / "வந்துச்சு" | "வருகிறது" / "வந்தது" | Don't mix tenses in formal writing |
| 4 | "நீ எங்க இருக்க" | "நீ எங்கே இருக்கிறாய்" | Add the case suffix and full conjugation |
| 5 | "என்னோட புத்தகம்" | "என்னுடைய புத்தகம்" | Possessive in writing uses -உடைய |
| 6 | "மழை வருதான்னு நினைக்கிறேன்" | "மழை வருகிறது என்று நினைக்கிறேன்" | Same fix as #1 |
| 7 | "சாப்பிடுங்கனு சொன்னாரு" | "சாப்பிடுங்கள் என்று சொன்னார்" | Honorific plural uses -கள்; reported speech uses என்று |

## When NOT to trust an automated Tamil grammar checker

Even the best Tamil grammar checker online free will get some things wrong:

- **Poetic license** — Sangam-style or song-lyric Tamil intentionally bends grammar
- **Code-mixed Tamil** — Tanglish sentences with English nouns may confuse the model
- **Regional dialects** — Madurai vs Coimbatore vs Jaffna Tamil have local forms
- **Domain terms** — technical, medical, or legal Tamil may not be in the dictionary

Treat suggestions as a strong second opinion, not absolute truth. Your judgment as the writer is final.
